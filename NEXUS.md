Description:
Adaptive Weapon Handling, rebuilt as an NVSE plugin. Same idea as the script
version — the weapon tilts and lowers with your camera angle — but it holds the
weapon where it should be instead of chasing how fast you moved the mouse.

Features:

You lower your weapon if you look up!
You lower your weapon if you look down!
You can shoot!
Relaxed pose on a hotkey — press Z and the weapon just rests!
Or it relaxes on its own after standing around for a while!
Firing, reloading or drawing snaps you back out of it instantly!
Looking around does not — the whole point is that it follows the camera!
Heavy weapons tilt less, because they are heavy!
Full MCM menu!
No ESP!

Works great with FPS Weapon Lowering﻿
Motion inertia is deliberately left out — B42 Inertia already does that job
better than I would!

Requirements:

xNVSE
FalloutNV.exe 1.4.0.525 (Steam or GOG)
MCM Extender (optional — for the menu)

Installation:

Remove the script version first! Both adjust the same two settings and will
fight each other.
Install with MO2
MCM-RU folder is optional Russian menu text

Enjoy! (◡‿◡)
