[size=5][b]Description:[/b][/size]
Adaptive Weapon Handling, rebuilt as an NVSE plugin. Same idea as the script version — the weapon tilts and lowers with your camera angle — but it holds the weapon where it should be instead of chasing how fast you moved the mouse.

[size=5][b]Features:[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]You lower your weapon if you look up!
[*]You lower your weapon if you look down!
[*]You can shoot!
[*]Relaxed pose on a hotkey — press Z and the weapon just rests!
[*]Or it relaxes on its own after standing around for a while!
[*]Firing, reloading or drawing snaps you back out of it instantly!
[*]Looking around does not — the whole point is that it follows the camera!
[*]Heavy weapons tilt less, because they are heavy!
[*]Full MCM menu!
[*]No ESP!
[/list]

Works great with FPS Weapon Lowering
Motion inertia is deliberately left out — B42 Inertia already does that job better than I would!

[size=5][b]Requirements:[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]xNVSE
[*]FalloutNV.exe 1.4.0.525 (Steam or GOG)
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/newvegas/mods/93642]MCM Extender[/url] (optional — for the menu)
[/list]

[size=5][b]Installation:[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Remove the script version first![/b] Both adjust the same two settings and will fight each other.
[*]Install with MO2
[*]MCM-RU folder is optional Russian menu text
[/list]

Enjoy! (◡‿◡)
